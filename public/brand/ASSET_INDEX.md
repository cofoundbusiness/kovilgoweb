## 5. Usage & Integrity Policy

1. **No Redrawing or Modification:** The custom handwritten KovilGo wordmark must never be recreated, skewed, or typeset in an alternate font.
2. **Clear Space:** Maintain generous breathing space around the logo equivalent to the height of the letter 'o' in KovilGo.
3. **Contrast:** Always ensure maximum contrast between the yellow wordmark (`#FDB913`) and underlying surfaces (preferably Primary Black `#080808` or deep imagery).
